About WPU StreamFest 2026

Welcome to WPU StreamFest 2026 – Neon Nights, where music, creativity, and unforgettable experiences collide! WPU StreamFest is more than just a music festival; it's a celebration of youth, freedom, and self-expression. Inspired by the vibrant energy of neon lights and the electric atmosphere of nighttime festivals, we're bringing together music lovers, Gen Z communities, and creative souls for an extraordinary experience. From electrifying live performances and incredible DJs to immersive neon-themed attractions, every moment is designed to make your night unforgettable.

But the party doesn't stop at entertainment. WPU StreamFest 2026 is committed to creating a more sustainable and convenient festival experience. Through our eco-friendly initiatives, we encourage visitors to reduce plastic waste, use reusable materials, and care for the environment. Our cashless payment system also makes every transaction faster, safer, and hassle-free. Whether you're here for the music, the vibes, or the memories, WPU StreamFest 2026 is where your neon nights begin!
